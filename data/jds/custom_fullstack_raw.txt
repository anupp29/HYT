Job Description: Senior Embedded Systems Engineer
Job Title: Senior Embedded Systems Engineer
Experience Required: 8–10 years
Employment Type: Full-time

Job Summary
We are seeking an experienced Embedded Systems Engineer specializing in firmware development, hardware interfacing, and real-time operating systems for automotive and industrial control systems.

Key Responsibilities
Develop embedded firmware using Embedded C and Assembly language.
Design and implement device drivers for microcontrollers and microprocessors.
Work with ARM Cortex-M, AVR, and PIC architectures.
Develop and debug firmware using JTAG, oscilloscopes, and logic analyzers.
Implement real-time applications using FreeRTOS, VxWorks, and Zephyr RTOS.
Design communication interfaces using CAN, LIN, SPI, I2C, and UART protocols.
Perform PCB-level debugging and hardware-software integration.
Ensure compliance with ISO 26262 and MISRA C coding standards.
Optimize firmware for memory usage, power consumption, and execution speed.
Lead embedded engineering teams and manage hardware product development.
Mandatory Technical Skills
Embedded C and Assembly
Microcontroller programming
ARM Cortex-M, AVR, and PIC
FreeRTOS, VxWorks, and Zephyr
Device driver development
PCB design and debugging
CAN, LIN, SPI, I2C, and UART
JTAG debugging tools
ISO 26262 and MISRA C
Hardware-software integration
Educational Qualifications
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or Embedded Systems Engineering.

Experience Requirements
Minimum 8 years of professional embedded firmware development.
At least 5 years of experience with automotive embedded systems.
Proven experience leading embedded product development teams.
Demonstrated expertise in real-time operating systems and hardware debugging.
